Take the belt for a drive

A continuously variable transmission has no fixed gears. A steel belt runs between two pulleys whose halves slide apart and together, so the ratio changes smoothly. Watch the diagram at the bottom: engine speed holds steady while road speed climbs.

Gamepad works too: triggers for pedals, left stick to steer, bumpers to step.

Transmission modes

  • DHolds the engine low for economy and lets revs rise with the pedal.
  • SHolds 2,500 rpm or more for quick response and stronger engine braking.
  • MSeven fixed ratio steps you choose, like a stepped gearbox.
  • NEngine disconnected from the wheels. Rev it freely.

Goals

  • Sprint: 0–100 km/h in under 9.0 s
  • Hypermiler: one full km at 5.5 L/100 km or less, averaging 70 km/h or more
  • Clean 5: 5 km without hitting traffic or leaving the road
Starting also turns on engine sound.
